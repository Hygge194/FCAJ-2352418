Sharing and Feedback

⚠️ Note: The information below is for reference purposes only. Please do not copy verbatim for your report, including this warning.

During my 2-month internship and journey with the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program, I would like to share my personal thoughts and feedback on my experience:

Overall Evaluation

1. Working Environment and Corporate Culture
The working environment in the program is truly open and professional. What impressed me the most was the enthusiasm of the mentors and the admin team; everyone was always ready to answer questions and help resolve issues, even after working hours. This made me feel supported and reassured that I wouldn’t get stuck when encountering technical difficulties.

2. Training Materials and Learning Roadmap
The program’s guidance materials are prepared extremely carefully and realistically. Each step has clear, detailed illustrations, which helped a beginner like me easily absorb and follow. In particular, the weekly Saturday event sharing sessions brought a lot of value, giving me opportunities to learn from the real-world experiences of the seniors.

3. Relevance to Career Orientation
The tasks and projects I worked on align well with my major at school. It did not only help me consolidate theory but also clearly shaped my future career path.

Points to Improve

Besides the great professional values, there is a minor point I hope the program could consider for future batches. Since the learning environment is mostly self-study and online communication, I hope the program can organize more offline networking sessions or group mini-projects. This would help participants connect, share experiences directly, and improve their teamwork skills.

Personal Thoughts

  • About the corporate environment: In my opinion, experiencing a corporate environment has broadened my horizon, helped me understand the working culture and manage my time rationally to meet deadlines. The environment here is very friendly and cooperative. As a large enterprise, there are sometimes strict and tight regulations, but because of that, I have learned a lot about the processes of a large company.
  • About the internship program: I feel that what the program and these projects bring is truly beneficial for me. The experience I accumulated during the internship and working on projects assigned by the program will definitely help me a lot in my future journey.
  • About myself: Interning this summer has made me realize I still have many weaknesses to overcome and gaps to fill. I truly appreciate the time interning here as it helped me recognize what I need to improve to become better in my career.
