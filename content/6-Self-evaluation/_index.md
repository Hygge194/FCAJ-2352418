During my internship at Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited (Bootcamp First Cloud AI Journey) from 15/06/2026 to 14/08/2026, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge acquired in school to a real-world working environment.  

### Skills and Knowledge Acquired
After 9 weeks of interning at the enterprise, I have learned and acquired many things from senior professionals in the field, specifically:
* Participated in a professional software development process, working according to established standards and effective methodologies.
* Learned about system development principles to scale systems on a large scale, allowing multiple people to participate in development without conflicts and allowing easy integration of new features without affecting existing system parts.
* Learned about systems and cloud solutions instead of on-premise data centers. Understood the benefits of cloud computing, how to optimize cloud usage (such as AWS EC2, S3, RDS, VPC), and especially how to design and test a system to ensure it meets standards.
* Experienced designing and building the Shopsflow Backend application, understanding system architecture of a real-world project, and being guided on database design and module integration.
* Received training in security and security principles during system development (such as Spring Security & JWT). Understood the benefits of dividing internal APIs and public APIs.
* Participated in knowledge sharing sessions about roles and jobs within the FCAJ program and how team members work together effectively using the Agile methodology.
* Applied knowledge learned from university courses during the internship, identifying areas of improvement and knowledge gaps that need addressing.
