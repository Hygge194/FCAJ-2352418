# AWS Study Tour & Workshop: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application

**Time:** July 04, 2026

**Location:** Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam Office, Ho Chi Minh City.

**Role:** Participant (Student)

### Key Contents and Activities

The event is a field trip combined with a specialized workshop organized by AWS Study Group in collaboration with Swinburne Vietnam at the AWS office. The program provides a practical perspective on the Cloud Computing and Data Engineering markets, along with career orientation for IT students.

Key highlights included:

- Overview of Cloud Computing development trends in Vietnam, enterprise Cloud-first strategies, and new recruitment requirements for students and Cloud engineers, especially regarding Cloud-native, Kubernetes, and AI.
- Sharing experiences in deploying Data Engineering systems in real-world environments, introducing the DNA Data Platform model comprising Ingestion, Processing, Storage, Governance, and Analytics components, while analyzing the differences between academic problems and Production systems.
- Discussing essential soft skills in a corporate environment such as professional communication, networking, asking proactive questions, and seeking development opportunities with a "Blue Ocean" mindset.
- Sharing perspectives on using AI in studying and working, emphasizing that AI is a tool to improve productivity but cannot replace the critical thinking and foundational knowledge of an engineer.
- Participating in a Q&A and direct networking session with experts from AWS Vietnam, Cloud Kinetics, and Renova Cloud to learn more about career paths and required skills in the Cloud industry.

### Outcomes and Values Gained

After attending the program, I have:

- Gained a deeper understanding of Cloud Computing development trends and enterprise recruitment needs in the era of digital transformation and AI application.
- Understood the differences between classroom exercises and real-world deployed systems, particularly regarding scalability, performance, security, and data governance requirements.
- Learned the process of building a complete data platform through the DNA Data Platform model and how to apply it to real-world projects on the AWS platform.
- Recognized the importance of building personal projects (Side Projects), developing a personal brand, and maintaining proactivity in studying and working through the Value × Visibility × Consistency mindset.
- Practiced using AI effectively, viewing it as a support tool rather than relying entirely on it, while always verifying results based on foundational knowledge.
- Had the opportunity to network with experts in the Cloud field, expanding my understanding of the practical working environment and getting a clearer orientation for my career development path in Cloud Computing and Data Engineering.
